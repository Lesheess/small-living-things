ROOMIE 0.9
A pair of eyes that lives in your menu bar and keeps an eye on you.
For macOS 13 or later, Apple silicon and Intel.


INSTALL

1. Drag Roomie into your Applications folder.

2. Double-click it. The first time, macOS will refuse to open it:
   Roomie is not registered with Apple yet. To let it in:

   macOS 15 or later
     Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down to the line
     about Roomie being blocked, click Open Anyway, and confirm.

   macOS 13 or 14
     Right-click (or Control-click) Roomie, choose Open, then Open again.

3. A black pill drops from the menu bar: choose who moves in,
   Madam or the Gentleman. The eyes live at the right side of your
   menu bar. No Dock icon, no window.


KEEP THEM AFTER A RESTART

Right-click the eyes and choose Open at Login.


WHAT THEY DO

Every 20 minutes at the screen     look at something far away for 20 seconds; a chime at the end
Every 30 minutes in the chair      a five-minute walk
Past half past midnight            bed

They hop out of the menu bar into the notch, and a black card opens
with their line. Let it be and it slips away; they ask again later.

You come back after a while        hearts in their eyes
You poke them                      they squash; the third time, they cry down your screen
You shake the mouse                dizzy
You leave the Mac alone            they doze off
The battery runs low               sad
The Mac runs hot                   a bead of sweat
You say you'll walk, and stay put   narrowed eyes and one dry remark
You put music on                   they dance

On a call or watching something full screen, they keep quiet.
For an hour of peace: right-click the eyes, Quiet for an Hour.
For fewer nudges for good: right-click, Nudges, Only Walks and Bed,
or None, Just Company (no nudges, just the eyes).
No dancing: right-click, Dancing, Off.
Right-click the eyes and choose Playground to see all of it on buttons.


WHAT THEY READ

The clock, how long since you last touched the Mac, where the pointer is,
that a click happened (not where), the battery level, how hot the Mac runs,
whether sound is coming out of the Mac, and whether Spotify or Apple Music
is playing (and who: they may say so).
Nothing on your screen, nothing you type. No network code at all.
macOS asks for no permission.


DANCING TO THE BEAT (if you want it)

On their own they dance in their own way. To dance on the beat of whatever
plays, the browser too: right-click the eyes, Dancing, To the Beat
(macOS 14.2 or later). macOS asks once to let Roomie hear your Mac's sound.
They keep only the last fifth of a second of it, to find the beat.
Nothing is recorded, nothing is saved, nothing leaves your Mac.
If no question appears: System Settings > Privacy & Security >
Screen & System Audio Recording > System Audio Recording Only > Roomie.


MOVING OUT

If you turned on Open at Login, right-click the eyes and turn it off.
Then choose Quit Roomie and drag Roomie to the Trash.
